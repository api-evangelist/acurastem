# AcuraStem

> AcuraStem, Inc. is a privately held, patient-based biotechnology company in Monrovia,
> California, developing therapeutics for neurodegenerative diseases — principally
> sporadic ALS (amyotrophic lateral sclerosis) and frontotemporal dementia (FTD). Its
> iNeuroRx platform builds disease models from patient-derived induced pluripotent stem
> cells (iPSC) instead of animal models and applies machine learning to target discovery.
> Lead antisense oligonucleotide candidate: AS-241 (PIKFYVE).
